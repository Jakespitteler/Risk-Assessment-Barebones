## Goal

Jake asked on 2026-11-20 to look at rejecting hazards that are made up or written in poor stock wording, the
same few words anyone could type for any job. Today the vague-hazard nudge (T6) only asks the worker to say more,
and the ranking (task 0032) only counts it against them afterwards.

## Decide first

Questions to investigate with Jake when it starts.

1. **Refuse, or push back harder?** Refusing blocks a worker on the job site; a stronger nudge doesn't. What
   happens when the AI is wrong?
2. **How is "made up" told apart?** A hazard that doesn't fit the job (falling from height for a desk job), one
   copied from the worker's last Take 5, or stock phrases common across many Take 5s.
3. **What counts as stock wording?** A fixed list, the AI's judgement, or the most repeated hazards in the saved
   Take 5s.
4. **How does it fit "own words first" and T6** (spec D1, D26 to D33, D56 to D63)?
